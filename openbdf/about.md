# OpenBDF - Open Building Data Format

**Version 2.0.0** | October 2026 | pre-release

> OpenBDF is developed by the team of GBDI Global Building Data Initiative at EPFL and RISE with technical support from EPFL ENAC-IT4R. This version is shared for expert review and is not yet validated. Any licence to use it is non-exclusive and limited to the purpose for which it was shared. While we welcome feedback and proposals to evolve the format, material changes to the specification are to be agreed with GBDI before they are published, so that the format remains coherent and adjustments can be consolidated ahead of releases.
